# Soldering-Fume-Extractor---Kirby
Fume Extractor to collect the fume while soldering.

Well apparently it's not very good or healthy to breath in the fumes that comes when soldering.
At first I was going to just buy one because that's quick and painless, but they aren't very cheap, even for basic fume extractors.
So I researched, and I discovered that there are very little components needed to make a fume extractor.
1. A fan that is strong enough to full air as well as push.
2. An Activated Carbon (Charcoal) filter.

And that's it. That's all we need for a basic fume extractor.
All I needed was a DC fan (12V) and the filter which were readily available on amazon.
After I received the packages, I measured and went to work to design a CAD model around the parameters of the fan on SolidWorks.
The housing can be very basic as long as it holds all the pieces together, but where's the fun in that when I can CAD anything I wanted for the housing.
So I went to CAD Kirby.
